Down payment assistance in Arizona

Arizona has programs that can help eligible buyers with the down payment or closing costs — for example Home Plus (Arizona Industrial Development Authority) and Home in Five Advantage (Maricopa County). Eligibility, income limits and terms change, so Lizbeth will connect you with a lender who can confirm what you qualify for.

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to sign an agreement with my agent before touring homes?

Yes. Since August 2024, REALTORS® work with buyers under a written agreement before touring. It explains the services and how compensation works — which is negotiable and may sometimes be paid by the seller as part of the negotiation. Lizbeth will walk you through it line by line.

How much do I need for a down payment?

It depends on the loan. Some conventional loans allow 3% down and FHA loans 3.5%; VA and USDA loans can offer 0% down to eligible buyers. You'll also need funds for closing costs, which assistance programs may help cover.

Can I buy a home with an ITIN?

Some lenders offer ITIN loans. Requirements vary, so Lizbeth can refer you to lenders who work with ITIN borrowers.

How long does it take to buy a home?

Once your offer is accepted, a financed purchase commonly closes in about 30–45 days. The home search before that depends on the market and what you're looking for.

What is earnest money?

It's a good-faith deposit you make when your offer is accepted. It's held by the title/escrow company and normally applied to your purchase at closing.
