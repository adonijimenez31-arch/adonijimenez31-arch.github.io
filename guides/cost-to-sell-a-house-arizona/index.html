What Does It Cost to Sell a House in Arizona?

Lizbeth GuzmánREALTOR® · Barrett Real EstateAZ License # SA714147000
Updated: 2026-10-29

Knowing your costs up front helps you price with confidence and know what you'll take home. Here are the usual items for a seller in Arizona.

Common selling costs

  • Brokerage compensation — always negotiable. Since 2024 buyer-agent compensation is negotiated separately, and sellers may choose to offer it or credits as part of the negotiation.
  • Title and escrow — by custom in Arizona the seller usually pays for the buyer's owner's title insurance policy, and escrow fees are often split.
  • HOA fees — if the home is in an HOA, there are usually transfer and disclosure fees.
  • Your mortgage payoff and any liens.
  • Prorated property taxes up to the closing date.
  • Repairs or credits agreed after the buyer's inspection.
  • Any concessions you agree to, such as help with the buyer's closing costs.

Arizona does not charge a state real estate transfer tax.

Estimate your net proceeds

Net proceeds = sale price − mortgage payoff − selling costs. Before you list, Lizbeth prepares an estimated net sheet for your home so there are no surprises.

Ways to net more

  • Price it right from day one — homes priced well attract more buyers.
  • Do the small things: clean, declutter, touch-up paint and curb appeal.
  • Fix inexpensive issues before buyers find them in the inspection.
  • Market to both English- and Spanish-speaking buyers.

General information, not legal or tax advice. Customs and fees vary by contract and title company; your escrow officer will provide the final figures.
